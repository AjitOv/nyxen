NYXEN — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 18, 2026 • Version 1.0

NYXEN ("the Application", "the Game") is a procedural 3D underwater simulation and Apex Predator Battle Royale web and mobile game. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to user data privacy and transparency.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

NYXEN is engineered from the ground up to operate completely client-side without collecting, tracking, harvesting, transmitting, or selling any personally identifiable information (PII). We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, IP logs, or device hardware identifiers.

2. Local On-Device Storage

The application utilizes browser local storage (localStorage) and Cache Storage exclusively on your local device to:

  • Save your current unlocked fish species selection and discovered landmarks.
  • Record your collected ocean pearls count and high scores.
  • Cache game engine assets via Service Worker for offline gameplay.

This data remains strictly on your device and is never synchronized to external servers or third-party networks. You can clear this data at any time via your browser settings.

3. Third-Party Analytics & Advertising

NYXEN contains:

  • No third-party advertising SDKs or trackers.
  • No user behavior profiling or tracking pixels.
  • No external telemetry networks.

4. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Because NYXEN does not collect any personal data whatsoever, it is fully safe for players of all ages and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
