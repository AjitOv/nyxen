🌊 Overview

NYXEN is an open-source, first-person underwater exploration simulation where players become biologically accurate marine fishes and explore a seamless infinite procedural ocean.

Built with modular JavaScript, WebGPU & WebGL2 fallback, and 100% procedural Web Audio synthesis.

🎮 Controls

  • Click Canvas: Lock mouse pointer for smooth analog 3D steering
  • W / S: Forward propulsion / reverse hydrodynamic drag
  • A / D: Strafe left / right with biological banking roll
  • Space / E: Ascend towards the surface
  • Q: Descend into the depths
  • Shift: Burst swim sprint (consumes stamina)
  • V: Toggle First-Person Eye Cam vs Third-Person Chase Cam
  • 1 - 8: Instant fish species quick-switch
  • M: Toggle procedural ocean soundscape
  • Tab / J: Open Marine Logbook & Encyclopedia

⚡ Performance & Network Transfer

Target initial transfer is below 2.0 MB. NYXEN achieves a complete initial experience transfer of 44.1 KB (Gzip) / 37.9 KB (Brotli), which is 97.8% below the budget limit.

Zero images, 3D model files, or recorded audio samples are downloaded; all meshes, shaders, ocean caustics, and sound effects are generated procedurally in real-time.
