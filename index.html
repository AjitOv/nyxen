🎮 Controls & Technical Architecture

DESKTOP CONTROLS

  • • Click Canvas: Lock mouse for smooth analog pitch/yaw look & steering
  • • W / S: Swim forward / reverse drag
  • • A / D: Strafe left / right with biological banking roll
  • • Space / E: Ascend towards the surface
  • • Q: Descend into the depths
  • • Shift: Burst swim (consumes stamina)
  • • V: Toggle First-Person Eye Cam / Third-Person Chase Cam
  • • 1 - 8: Instant fish character quick-switch
  • • M: Toggle procedural Web Audio soundscape
  • • Tab / J: Open Marine Logbook & Encyclopedia

TOUCH CONTROLS (MOBILE)

  • • Left Screen Touch: Drag to swim forward/backward/strafe
  • • Right Screen Touch: Drag to steer pitch and yaw
  • • BURST Button: Speed boost sprint
